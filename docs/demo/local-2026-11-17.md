## What this shows

- **Answers come from the record, with sources.** The diabetes date, the HbA1c trend and the four current medications
  are each cited to the FHIR resource they come from.
- **No answer is invented.** Asthma is not in patient 7945's record, and the answer says so.
- **Known limitations, on the record.** Patient 2077 has prediabetes and a kidney disorder due to diabetes but no plain
  diabetes diagnosis; the model answers "not recorded" without mentioning the related records, which is correct
  but less helpful than it could be. The summary includes Synthea's social entries (employment is coded as a
  condition in this data) and leaves HbA1c out of its key labs. Both are next refinements.
